# Main recipe quality audit — batch 7

Audit date: 2026-09-25

Scope: the next 15 built-in main dishes, `main-121` through `main-135`. This is a code and culinary plausibility review, not a claim that the recipes have been cooked in a test kitchen.

## Standard applied

Every recipe was checked for realistic four-person quantities, ingredient-direction agreement, full elapsed time for a normal home cook, heat and doneness guidance, beginner clarity, common failure points, food waste, Hungarian-English parity, allergens, and a warm but concise tone.

## Main corrections

- Trout, lamb, turkey, pork, tuna patties, and broccoli patties now use measurable safe-temperature guidance instead of unreliable colour alone.
- Mushroom and walnut ragout now lists its water and browns 700 g mushrooms in batches instead of steaming an overcrowded pan.
- Tofu and aubergine stir-fry has enough oil for the stated cooking method.
- Cauliflower, aubergine steaks, squash, chickpeas, and kale use two trays where one tray would prevent browning.
- Red lentils are checked from eight minutes so the salad does not become mushy.
- The 800 g pork quantity is handled as two realistic tenderloins, with the filling divided and a 63 °C check before resting.
- Canned tuna and chickpeas now show purchased weights and explain the drained yield, preventing shopping-list under-buying.
- Tuna and broccoli patties include realistic batch-cooking time and oil allocation.
- All 15 recipes now have explicit preparation, cooking, resting, total-time, difficulty, allergen, translation, and audit metadata.

## Remaining evidence

All recipes remain `code-reviewed`, not `kitchen-verified`. Priority dishes should be cooked first because fish thickness, mushroom pan size, aubergine oil uptake, lentil variety, kohlrabi age, meatball size, stuffed-pork thickness, patty binding, cabbage-leaf size, and multi-tray browning cannot be proven by automated tests or text review alone.
