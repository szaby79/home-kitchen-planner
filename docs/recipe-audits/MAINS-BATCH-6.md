# Main recipe quality audit — batch 6

Audit date: 2026-09-25

Scope: the next 15 built-in main dishes, `main-106` through `main-120`. This is a code and culinary plausibility review, not a claim that the recipes have been cooked in a test kitchen.

## Standard applied

Every recipe was checked for realistic four-person quantities, ingredient-direction agreement, full elapsed time for a normal home cook, heat and doneness guidance, beginner clarity, common failure points, food waste, Hungarian-English parity, allergens, and a warm but concise tone.

## Main corrections

- Chicken, ground beef, pork, turkey, and cod now use measurable safe-temperature guidance rather than colour alone; required resting time is included where relevant.
- Canned white beans now show the purchased can weight, explain the drained yield, and list the water used in the method.
- Stuffed zucchini boats are pre-baked so the shells soften reliably before the filling dries out.
- Buckwheat uses controlled stock rather than an excessive all-at-once quantity, with a clear texture cue and reserved liquid for adjustment.
- Zucchini lasagna now pre-bakes the slices and drains the mozzarella to reduce excess water; its longer real elapsed time includes the preparation and rest.
- Caprese mushrooms, tempeh, halloumi, and the vegetable dishes use friendlier serving guidance without adding filler.
- All 15 recipes now have explicit preparation, cooking, resting, total-time, difficulty, allergen, translation, and audit metadata.
