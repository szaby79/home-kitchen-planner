# Main recipe quality audit — batch 2

Audit date: 2026-09-25

Scope: the next 15 built-in recipes whose category is actually `main`: `main-16` through `main-23`, then `main-28` through `main-34`. IDs `main-24` through `main-27` are creamed vegetable stews and are deliberately reserved for that category's audit.

This is a code and culinary plausibility review, not a claim that the recipes have been cooked in a test kitchen.

## Standard applied

Every recipe was checked for realistic four-person quantities, ingredient-direction agreement, complete elapsed time for a normal home cook, heat and doneness guidance, beginner clarity, common failure points, Hungarian-English parity, allergens, and a warm but concise tone.

## Main corrections

- Hungarian and English methods now agree on temperature, duration, rest, water quantities, sequence, and doneness cues.
- Roast pork ribs retain the full two-hour covered roast plus browning and resting time; the honey glaze includes a burn warning.
- Paprika-based dishes explicitly remove the pan from the heat before adding paprika.
- Cabbage noodles include the twenty-minute salting rest in total elapsed time.
- Tomato cabbage now cooks its rice separately in both languages instead of giving conflicting methods.
- Hake and breaded catfish include bone warnings, realistic shallow-frying guidance, and visual doneness cues.
- Breaded catfish now has the milk, butter, and sufficient potatoes required for its stated mashed-potato side.
- Hortobágyi savoury crêpes use the same egg count in the ingredient list and method, and include batter resting in total time.
- Milanese pork is no longer plain tomato pasta: the characteristic ham-and-mushroom sauce is included, and the crisp cutlet is served beside it.
- New notes have proper English translations, while all recipes remain marked `code-reviewed` pending kitchen verification.
