# Main recipe quality audit — batch 3

Audit date: 2026-09-25

Scope: 15 built-in main dishes, `main-35` through `main-49`. This is a code and culinary plausibility review, not a claim that the recipes have been cooked in a test kitchen.

## Standard applied

Every recipe was checked for realistic four-person quantities, ingredient-direction agreement, full elapsed time for a normal home cook, heat and doneness guidance, beginner clarity, common failure points, Hungarian-English parity, allergens, and a warm but concise tone.

## Main corrections

- Cigánypecsenye now explains how to prepare the listed potato side and protects paprika from burning.
- Mátrai borzaska has enough oil for the stated shallow-frying method and includes a pork doneness check.
- Vadas now includes the sugar and lemon required to balance its characteristic sweet-sour sauce, plus properly seasoned dumplings.
- The 1.2 kg pork hock receives a realistic 90–105 minute pre-cook, tenderness cue, and resting time.
- Layered savoy cabbage and cauliflower have realistic four-person rice ratios and matching Hungarian-English oven instructions.
- Tarhonyás hús uses the same 600 ml water and 45–55 minute pork braise in both languages.
- Bácskai rice and pork uses measured water adjusted for the remaining stew liquid instead of the unreliable “one centimetre above” instruction.
- Slambuc now states the duration and visual result of its final uncovered stage.
- Egg dumplings carry enough listed salt for the dough, dressing, eggs, and cooking water.
- All 15 recipes now have explicit timing, difficulty, allergen, note-translation, and audit metadata.
