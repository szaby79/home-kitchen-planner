# Main recipe quality audit — batch 4

Audit date: 2026-09-25

Scope: the next 15 built-in main dishes: `main-50` and `main-57` through `main-70`. Recipes `main-51` through `main-56` belong to the separate `stew` category and are intentionally reserved for that category audit. This is a code and culinary plausibility review, not a claim that the recipes have been cooked in a test kitchen.

## Standard applied

Every recipe was checked for realistic four-person quantities, ingredient-direction agreement, full elapsed time for a normal home cook, heat and doneness guidance, beginner clarity, common failure points, Hungarian-English parity, allergens, and a warm but concise tone.

## Main corrections

- Dödölle now lists enough salt for the potato mixture and cooking water, with a friendlier serving cue.
- Breaded cauliflower gives a real frying time, batch guidance, and enough cooling time before breading.
- Stuffed chicken now uses all the listed butter, allows a realistic roast, and requires both thigh and stuffing to reach 74 °C before its 15-minute rest.
- Duck and goose legs now use matching temperatures and times in both languages, with tenderness cues, crisping stages, and rests.
- Catfish paprikash allocates the sour cream between sauce and noodles, explains the bacon step, and begins with a bone check.
- Rác carp now has one consistent bilingual layering and covered/uncovered baking method, plus a clear small-bone warning.
- Pork dishes no longer promise rice, egg barley, nokedli, or potatoes that are absent from their ingredient lists.
- Butcher’s-style pork receives a realistic 45–55 minute braise and is salted only after the salty ham and pickles are added.
- Chicken liver includes the previously missing potato method and a thermometer-based 74 °C safety check because colour alone is unreliable.
- Tripe, pork trotters, and rooster retain genuinely long home-cooking times with tenderness cues instead of optimistic fixed claims.
- All 15 recipes now have explicit timing, difficulty, allergen, note-translation, and audit metadata.

## Remaining evidence

All recipes remain `code-reviewed`, not `kitchen-verified`. Priority dishes should be cooked first because stuffed-poultry heat penetration, liver doneness, fish bones, breading adhesion, long-braised tenderness, dumpling texture, and final sauce balance cannot be proven by automated tests or text review alone.
