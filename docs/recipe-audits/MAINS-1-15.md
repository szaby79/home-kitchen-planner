# Main recipe quality audit — batch 1

Audit date: 2026-09-25

Scope: the first 15 built-in main dishes (`main-1` through `main-15`). This is a code and culinary plausibility review, not a claim that the recipes have been cooked in a test kitchen.

## Standard applied

Each main was checked for realistic four-person quantities, ingredient-direction agreement, full elapsed time, heat and doneness guidance, beginner clarity, common failure points, Hungarian-English parity, allergens, and a warm but concise tone.

## Main corrections

- Frying recipes now list enough oil for the stated shallow-frying method.
- Nokedli recipes include enough salt for both the main dish and the dumpling dough.
- Rakott krumpli and rakott káposzta were reduced from roughly six-person quantities to realistic four-person amounts.
- The rice ratio in töltött káposzta and töltött paprika was corrected so the filling is not dominated by rice.
- Töltött káposzta now lists the whole sauerkraut leaves required for rolling, separately from the shredded sauerkraut.
- Hungarian and English methods now agree on cooking temperatures, durations, rice preparation, cabbage rolls and serving details.
- Rántott sajt includes its real chilling time; casseroles and meatloaf include required resting time.
- Brassói cooking time was corrected, its frying oil made realistic, and purchased pickles are served flexibly by size rather than treated as a fixed cooking step.
- Ground meat, chicken and pork recipes include practical doneness checks.
