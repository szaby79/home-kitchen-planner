# Main recipe quality audit — batch 5

Audit date: 2026-09-25

Scope: the next 15 built-in main dishes, `main-91` through `main-105`. Recipes `main-71` through `main-90` belong to the separate `stew` category and are intentionally reserved for that category audit. This is a code and culinary plausibility review, not a claim that the recipes have been cooked in a test kitchen.

## Standard applied

Every recipe was checked for realistic four-person quantities, ingredient-direction agreement, full elapsed time for a normal home cook, heat and doneness guidance, beginner clarity, common failure points, food waste, Hungarian-English parity, allergens, and a warm but concise tone.

## Main corrections

- Canned chickpeas and black beans now show the actual purchased can weight and explain the drained yield, so the shopping list does not under-buy.
- Mushroom-lentil ragout uses a wide pan for browning, while reserved cauliflower cooking water makes the purée creamy without adding an unlisted ingredient.
- Stuffed sweet potatoes allow 45–60 minutes depending on size instead of promising that every potato will soften in 40 minutes.
- The halloumi tray bake and eggplant curry have enough listed oil for the vegetables they contain.
- Chicken, ground turkey, salmon, and the egg-based soufflé use measurable safe-temperature guidance instead of relying only on colour.
- The quinoa dish uses controlled liquid and tells the cook how to correct it if the quinoa needs more water.
- Lentil patties and vegetables use separate trays, avoiding the steaming and poor browning caused by crowding.
- Cauliflower steaks now use the loose florets from both heads rather than silently wasting most of the listed cauliflower.
- The ricotta soufflé now separates and folds the eggs properly, so the method produces an actual aerated bake instead of a dense casserole.
- All 15 recipes now have explicit preparation, cooking, resting, total-time, difficulty, allergen, translation, and audit metadata.

## Remaining evidence

All recipes remain `code-reviewed`, not `kitchen-verified`. Priority dishes should be cooked first because purée texture, sweet-potato size variation, salmon thickness, meatball size, quinoa hydration, vegan patty binding, multi-tray browning, and soufflé lift cannot be proven by automated tests or text review alone.
